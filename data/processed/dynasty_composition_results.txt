================================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================================================
Positive coefficient on dynasty variable -> dynastic LGUs spend MORE
  on this sector as a share of total CO spending.
Negative coefficient -> dynastic LGUs spend LESS on this sector.

If dynasties shift spending AWAY from health/education/social welfare
  and TOWARD general public services / economic services, this supports
  the capture-and-misallocate hypothesis (Paper 2 motivation).
